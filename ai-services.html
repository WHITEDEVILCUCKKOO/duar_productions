Why Modern Brands are Moving Away from Traditional Shoots.

Old-fashioned video filmmaking approaches require cameras, directors, lights, locations, actors and countless post-production revisions. This is nearly impossible for fast-moving startups, digital marketing agencies or any company that needs to create video content at scale and speed. Moving to high quality video production powered by artificial intelligence will deliver your organization with transformational structural benefits that completely change the economics of video production for your company.

  • Significant Reductions in Cost - Save between 70–80% versus traditional video production by eliminating studios, casting, and set construction from your process.
  • Extremely Fast Turnaround Times - Traditional ad agencies typically deliver a finished marketing video in 3–6 weeks. With use of specialized AI systems, we will have your first drafts of high definition finished video ready for you to review in 48–72 hours.
  • Unlimited Scalability and Revisions - Need to add features to a product or change pricing (while doing a marketing campaign)? Rather than incur expensive new production costs to reshoot video, we will modify the code, script or base image of your previously completed video content.
  • Hyper-Personalization - With our technology, we can easily create hundreds of variations (each targeted toward specific customer profiles) of your marketing material for use in performance based, hyper-targeted digital marketing campaigns.
