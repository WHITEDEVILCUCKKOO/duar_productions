Analysis & Casting

You provide us with your script and brief. We review the need and send a playlist of the voice samples that best fit your vision. You pick the voice you love.

The Recording Session

We capture the session in our sound-proof voice recording studio live. Clients can also choose to "Direct the Session" (remotely via Zoom/Skype/Source-Connect) allowing real-time feedback from the artist.

Post-Production & QC

The raw audio is cleaned (de-breathing / EQ / compression). Missed words, mispronunciations, or background noise are spotted against the script by our Quality Control (QC) team.

Delivery & Feedback

We get these quality files to you in the format of your choice (WAV, MP3, AIFF). If you require a tonal adjustment or correction, we make revisions quickly.
